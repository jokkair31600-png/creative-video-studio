# Creative Video Studio

## AI-Assisted Video Creation

Creative Video Studio is an independent personal project designed to create, edit and prepare original video content for social media.

## Planned features

- AI-assisted script generation.
- AI-generated images and videos.
- Automated video editing.
- Title, description and hashtag generation.
- Human approval before publication.
- TikTok content upload integration.

## Technology

The project uses n8n, Python, FFmpeg, Qwen, Agnes AI and Telegram.

## Development status

Creative Video Studio is currently under development and restricted to authorized testing accounts.
