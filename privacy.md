# Creative Video Studio – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 24, 2026**

## 1. Overview

Creative Video Studio is an independent personal video creation and automation project currently under development.

## 2. Data processed

Depending on the enabled integrations and permissions, the application may process:

- TikTok account identifiers and basic profile information.
- Authentication and authorization tokens.
- Video content, descriptions and hashtags.
- Technical logs and publication status.
- Content approval decisions.

## 3. Purpose of processing

Data is processed to authenticate authorized accounts, generate and prepare video content, manage human approval and submit approved content to TikTok.

## 4. Third-party services

The application may use TikTok, Telegram, Agnes AI, GitHub and Cloudflare. These services process information according to their respective privacy policies.

## 5. Data storage and security

The application is designed to store project data locally and restrict access to authentication credentials. Sensitive credentials must not be published in public repositories.

## 6. Data sharing

The project does not sell personal data. Information may be transmitted to integrated services as necessary to perform requested functions.

## 7. Data retention

Data should be retained only for as long as necessary for the operation, security and testing of the application.

## 8. User rights

Users may request access, correction or deletion of their personal data and withdraw application permissions through their TikTok account settings.

## 9. Contact

General privacy inquiries may be submitted through the project's GitHub Issues section. Sensitive personal information should not be posted publicly.

## 10. Policy updates

This privacy policy may be updated as the application evolves.
